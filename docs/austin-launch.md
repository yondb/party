# Austin launch — marketing hooks

**Position:** Dog walks & outdoor crews in Austin — not “another sports map.”

## Copy (EN)

- Hero: *Find your crew for dog walks, runs, and parks in Austin.*
- CTA: *Create a meetup → share the link → fill your party.*
- Dog: *Sunday Zilker loop — friendly dogs welcome.*

## Channels (week 1)

1. r/Austin, r/AustinPets, r/dogs — one post + link `?utm_source=reddit`
2. FB: “Austin Dog Owners”, “ATX Dogs” — seed 5 `dog_walk` slots first
3. 3 Reels/TikTok: map pin → “create crew” → share button demo
4. Nextdoor (1 neighborhood): dog park meetup invite

## Seed slots (manual)

Create 5–10 `dog_walk` at: Zilker, Auditorium Shores, Red Bud Isle, Mueller Lake Park, Shoal Creek.

## Metrics

Share clicks (UTM), signups, `slot_created` — target 1 share / 5 slots in 30 days.
